Why LogBERT?

Traditional rule-based and statistical methods fail on modern log data. LogBERT understands the language of logs.

🧠

Contextual Understanding

LogBERT reads each log message in context — the same word means something different in different positions. Rule-based systems cannot capture this.

🔗

Sequence Awareness

Anomalies often appear as unusual sequences of normal-looking events. LogBERT detects patterns across consecutive log entries, not just individual lines.

🏷️

No Label Dependency

Keyword-based labeling causes data leakage. LogBERT learns from raw log structure without memorising labeling rules.

🌐

Real-World Generalisation

Pre-trained on billions of tokens, LogBERT transfers knowledge to unseen log formats without retraining from scratch.

⚡

Production Speed

Inference runs in milliseconds per log entry. The AWS Lambda deployment processes a 430-entry CSV in under 2.4 seconds.

🔍

Explainable Outputs

Each anomaly detection includes a probability score and matched keywords so administrators understand why a log was flagged.
